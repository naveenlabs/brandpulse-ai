A combined report

Reading a combined report needs JavaScript, and this browser is not running it. The sweep itself is plain JSON on this machine and the API that serves it is still reachable under /sweeps/.

What a combined score is not

It is the mean of five single-video scores, so it inherits every caveat above and adds one of its own: five videos is far too few to generalise from, and the range those five covered matters more than what they average to. A combined score is not a measurement of the subject. It is a statement about five particular videos, scored by one controller model on one day.
