What the scores are

Authenticity is 100 minus the weighted mean cross-channel conflict, with flagged segments counted double. Brand Health is 60% audience comment sentiment and 40% authenticity. Both measure how far four independent readings of the same moment disagree. Neither is a finding about whether the speaker was telling the truth, and neither is comparable between videos.
