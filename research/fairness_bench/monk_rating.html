Monk skin tone rating — one value per speaker

The scale is not reproduced here, on purpose. Open the official Monk Skin Tone Scale in another window and compare against that. Reproducing ten colour swatches from memory into the instrument that measures colour would introduce exactly the error this bench exists to look for — so this tool takes a number and shows you faces, and nothing else.
