How to rate (read once)
  • Judge only the largest face in the frame. Ignore small background faces.
  • A face on a screen inside the shot still counts as a face.
  • Judge this still alone. Don't try to remember the video.
  • Most frames will be Neutral. That is expected — don't hunt for emotion.
  • Angry and Sad are separate on purpose. Anger = tense, irritated. Sad = let down, deflated. If it's genuinely between them, use them as you see them; don't split the difference.
  • Click the image to zoom. Faces can be small in a 4K frame.
  • Use Can't tell freely. A guess is worse than an abstention here.
  • Some frames repeat. That is deliberate and it is measuring consistency — just rate what you see, don't try to recall your earlier answer.
