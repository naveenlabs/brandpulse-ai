# August 2026 pilot instrument

> **Superseded instrument.** This file records the four questions used in the
> August 2026 screenshot pilot. It is retained as a record of the method and is
> not the current evaluation instrument. The proposed follow-up instrument is
> `PILOT_QUESTIONS_V2.md`; consent documentation is in `CONSENT.md`.

## Study design

- **Date:** 2 August 2026
- **Participants:** three people known to the researcher
- **Duration:** approximately 15 minutes per participant
- **Format:** remote review of seven interface screenshots
- **Purpose:** identify recurring comprehension problems before the main evaluation

The screenshots showed the landing page and the overview, segment and comment
views for one high-conflict report and one lower-conflict comparison report.
Participants reviewed the material independently and received no explanation of
the scores before answering.

## Questions used

1. **Without any explanation, what do you think this dashboard is telling you?**
   Participants were asked to describe uncertain or guessed interpretations.

2. **What is confusing or unclear, or made you wonder what something meant?**
   Participants were encouraged to identify a specific number or section.

3. **If you were deciding whether to trust a sponsored review using this
   dashboard, what additional information would you want to see?**

4. **How easy was the dashboard to understand overall?**
   Responses used a five-point scale, where 1 meant completely confusing and 5
   meant completely clear.

## Analysis rule

Answers were recorded under participant identifiers. An observation was treated
as a recurring pattern only when at least two participants raised it
independently. Individual observations were retained separately and were not
presented as shared findings.
